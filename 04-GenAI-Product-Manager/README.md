# 📐 Track 4 — GenAI Product Manager

> 🔒 **Status: Scaffolded.** This track's full lessons and examples are being built next. This README is a placeholder outlining what's coming.

## What you'll learn

1. **AI Product Strategy and Lifecycle** — from idea to launch, build-vs-buy, roadmapping AI features.
2. **Identifying and Validating AI Use Cases** — spotting good GenAI fits, feasibility, ROI framing.
3. **Model and Prompt Evaluation Metrics** — accuracy, hallucination rate, latency/cost tradeoffs, human eval.
4. **AI User Experience and Human-in-the-loop Design** — trust, transparency, failure-state UX.
5. **Governance, Compliance, and Risk Management** — policy, data governance, responsible-AI review processes.

## Prerequisite

Complete [Track 1 — Foundations & Orientation](../01-Foundations-and-Orientation/README.md) first. Tracks 2 and 3 are recommended but optional for the PM path.
