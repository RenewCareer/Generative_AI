# Refund Policy

## Return window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in the original packaging.

## Exceptions
Clearance items and gift cards are final sale and cannot be refunded. Opened software licences are also non-refundable.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Store credit is issued instantly if the customer prefers it.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, the customer should report it within 7 days and include a photo. Acme pays the return shipping for damaged items.
