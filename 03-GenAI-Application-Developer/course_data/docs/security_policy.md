# Information Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all staff accounts.

## Reporting incidents
Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Customer data
Customer personal data must never be pasted into external AI tools. Only approved internal assistants may process it.

## Laptops
Company laptops must use full disk encryption and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
