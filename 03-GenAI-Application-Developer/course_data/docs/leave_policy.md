# Employee Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave each year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave, and other parents receive 6 weeks of paid leave.

## Requesting leave
Leave requests are submitted in the PeopleHub portal at least 14 days in advance whenever possible.
