# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars.

## Free shipping
Orders above 75 dollars qualify for free standard delivery.

## International orders
International shipping is available to 40 countries and takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties are paid by the customer.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch. Error code SHP-404 on the tracking page means that the courier has not scanned the parcel yet.
