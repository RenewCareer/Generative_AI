# Product Warranty

## Coverage
All Acme electronics carry a 2 year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Accessories such as cables and cases carry a 6 month warranty.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or repairs carried out by unauthorised shops.

## Making a claim
Customers open a claim on the support portal with the serial number and proof of purchase. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days.
