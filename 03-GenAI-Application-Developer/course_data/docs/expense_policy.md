# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
Meal expenses are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day while travelling for work. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 180 dollars per night. Stays above that limit need written approval from a director.

## Receipts
Itemised receipts are required for every expense above 25 dollars. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

## Flights
Economy class is standard. Business class is allowed only on flights longer than 8 hours, with approval from a vice president.
