# Product FAQ

## Aurora X2 headphones
The Aurora X2 battery lasts 40 hours per charge. To pair the headphones, hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue.

## Nimbus smart speaker
To reset the Nimbus speaker, hold the mute button for 15 seconds. Error code NB-17 means that the speaker has lost its Wi-Fi connection.

## Terra fitness band
The Terra band is water resistant to 50 metres. It syncs with both Android and iOS through the Acme Fit app.
