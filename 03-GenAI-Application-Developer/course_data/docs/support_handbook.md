# Support Agent Handbook

## Response targets
Priority 1 tickets get a first response within 1 hour. Priority 2 tickets get a first response within 8 hours, and priority 3 tickets within 2 business days.

## Escalation
Tickets are escalated to a team lead after 2 failed resolution attempts. Any legal threat goes straight to the legal team.

## Goodwill credits
Support agents may issue goodwill credits of up to 50 dollars without approval. Larger credits need team lead approval.
